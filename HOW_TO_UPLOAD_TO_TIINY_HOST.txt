========================================================================
HOW TO UPLOAD TO TIINY HOST (tiiny.host) IN 3 SIMPLE STEPS
========================================================================

You have the ready-to-upload zip file right here:
File: aaa.zip (or tinyhost_farhana_portfolio.zip)

STEP 1: Open Tiiny Host
-----------------------
Go to your browser and visit:
https://tiiny.host

STEP 2: Drag and Drop the ZIP file
----------------------------------
- Take the file "aaa.zip" from this folder (or from your Desktop).
- Drag and drop it into the "Upload your file" box on tiiny.host.

STEP 3: Name your site and Publish
----------------------------------
- Enter a link name (for example: farhana-portfolio).
- Click "Publish".
- Your portfolio website is now LIVE on the internet!
